Keepsie

A tidier camera roll, one swipe at a time

Keepsie is an iPhone and iPad app for sorting your photos. Swipe to keep a photo or set it aside, a few each day, with a little cat for company. Nothing is deleted until you say so.

Help & support Privacy policy Terms of use

Your photos stay yours

Keepsie never uploads your photos. It works on your device and only remembers which photos you kept or set aside. There are no accounts to create.
