Keepsie

A tidier camera roll, one swipe at a time

Keepsie is a photo cleaner for iPhone and iPad. Swipe to keep a photo or set it aside, a few each day, with a little cat for company. It finds duplicates, similar and blurry shots, screenshots and videos, so you can free up storage. Nothing is deleted until you say so.

Help & support Privacy policy Terms of use

Your photos stay yours

Keepsie never uploads your photos. It works on your device and only remembers which photos you kept or set aside. There are no accounts to create.
